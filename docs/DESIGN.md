# Studiply — product design (desktop + iPhone)

Designed Oct 2026. This file describes the screens and interactions; `SUMMARY-SPEC.md` describes what goes into a summary and exactly how it is calculated (the 10 sliders, derived variables, mark bands, quality checks, JSON schemas). Both use the app palette in `src/constants/colors.ts`. If they ever disagree, ask Lea.

## Layout (desktop, ≥ 1024 px wide)
The mockups in `docs/design/` show how each screen **looks** (desktop-01 recording, desktop-04 library, desktop-05 settings, iphone-01…); this file says how it **works**.

A top bar over up to three columns, on the full gradient (`Colors.gradient`, top to bottom), with content on white cards:
1. **Top bar** (white, 56 px): logo (gradient tile) + "Studiply", then depends on the screen (below), avatar at the right.
2. **Left sidebar (library, 264 px):** New button, Recent, Favorites, Exam packs, Review today (count badge), **Courses · drop files here** with a color square and count per course; the open course expands to show its files. Trash at the bottom. Every course and Trash is a drop target. On documents the search field sits here (under New).
3. **Center:** library screens use the full width; documents and settings sit in a centered column. A document has tabs `My notes · Summary · Transcript`, Export PDF, a Google-Docs-style formatting toolbar, and the page (white card, max width 820 px).
4. **Right panel (study assistant, 330 px):** Claude-style chat, **beside documents only**.

**Sidebar data (step 2.2):** Recent, Favorites and Exam packs open Library at that section (`/library?section=recent|favorites|exam-packs`); Review today opens Review and its badge counts reviews due by the end of today (hidden when 0, and offline). A course's count is its items of every kind (lectures, PDFs, images, …), not in Trash. The open course (the one the current screen or document is in) expands to its folders and files: a chevron opens a folder; the folder the student is in opens on the way down; the open item is highlighted. Right-click a course or an item in the sidebar for its menu (Open, Add to / Remove from Favorites). Clicking an item opens it where it lives: a lecture its notes (or post-session before it has notes), a folder its course at that folder (`/project/<id>?folder=<folder>`), and a PDF or image its course at its folder until files get a viewer.

What the shell shows depends on the kind of screen:

| Screen | Top bar | Sidebar | Assistant |
|---|---|---|---|
| Library (My library, a course, Search, Review) | wide salmon search field, red **● Record** button (VoiceOver: "Record lecture") | yes | no |
| Document (a note, recording) | breadcrumb `Course › folder › …` (each crumb opens the course at that level), document title, "Saved · 10:31" / "Saving…" / "Not saved", assistant toggle, Share | yes | yes |
| Settings | "‹ Back to library" and the page title | no | no |

The sidebar (☰ in the top bar) and the assistant (✦ in the top bar, × in the panel) collapse; each device remembers what is open. Below 1024 px the web shows the iPhone layout (tab bar, documents full screen); resizing switches live and keeps the open screen. A recording strip (Phase 5) sits between the top bar and the columns while recording.

On iPhone the same pieces stack: library is its own screen, the document is full screen, the assistant opens as a sheet. Full details in the **iPhone** section below. Every feature exists on both; the feature table in `BUILD-PLAN.md` ("Every feature, on iPhone and laptop") says how each one works on each device and which are v1 vs later.

## iPhone
The iPhone app is the main recording device (App Store). Same data, same features, adapted to one hand and a small screen.

**Navigation:** a white bottom tab bar **Library · Search · ● Record · Review · Settings** (docs/design iphone-01). Record is a big raised red circle (`Colors.recording`) with a white ring, exactly in the middle; the active tab is accent purple. Review holds quizzes, flashcards and Review today (a placeholder until the study phase). There is no Home screen: the app opens on Library. Documents and the recording flow (after its setup screen) open full screen without the tab bar, with a back button.

**Library:** on the gradient: big "Library" title, a dark round **+** (New) and the avatar; a salmon search field; chips Recent · Favorites · Exam packs (each shows that section's list in place of the overview; tap again to go back) · Review today (opens Review, shows the due count); **Courses** as a white card list (color square, name, count, chevron); **Recent** as small white cards of every kind (title, "Today · Summary ready", "Monday · PDF"); hint "Long-press any item to rename, move or add it to Favorites". Later: list or grid toggle; course folders with color dots; sections Recent, Favorites, Exam packs, Review today (badge), Trash. Long-press a file → menu (Open, Rename, Move to…, Move out, Add to Favorites, Summarize / Exam pack, Trash) and lift-to-drag onto a folder. Long-press empty space or the "…" button → New folder, New document, New recording, Upload slides/PDF, Paste, Sort. Undo toast after every move.

**Recording screen (portrait):**
- Top: course › lecture title, red dot + timer, live waveform.
- Middle: My notes editor (each new line gets a timestamp chip). The keyboard toolbar sits above the keyboard.
- Bottom bar, large thumb targets (≥ 56 px): ★ Important · ? Confused · Exam · Camera · Stop & summarize. Tapping a mark opens a small sheet: question, 1–10 slider with labeled ends, optional note, Save mark (light haptic on save).
- Assistant button opens the chat as a **half-height sheet** so notes stay visible; "Recording continues" badge; drag up for full height.
- Recording keeps running with the screen locked, in the background, during calls to the assistant, and without internet (chunks upload later).
- Later: Lock Screen / Dynamic Island Live Activity with timer and mark buttons; Action Button / Siri Shortcut to start recording.

**Document screen:** segmented control My notes · Summary · Transcript; "Aa" button opens a sheet with text styles (Title, Heading 1, Heading 2, Subheading, Normal, Quote) and size; keyboard toolbar (scrolls sideways): bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, color, highlight, lists, checklist, link; "+" menu for image, table, formula, comment, photo. Selecting text shows the iOS edit menu plus Ask Claude actions. Tapping a timestamp opens a mini audio player at the bottom. Share button → PDF via the iOS share sheet.

**Stop & summarize:** full-screen sheet: resources (notes locked on), the 10 sliders stacked in one column, presets as horizontal chips, Save as course default, then Generate. Progress shown while generating; the result opens in the Summary tab.

**Study:** Quiz me (tap to reveal), flashcards (swipe), push notifications for spaced review.

**System:** Dynamic Type, dark mode, VoiceOver labels on every control, permission prompts with clear reasons (microphone, camera, photos, notifications).

## Formatting toolbar
Undo, redo · text style (Title, Heading 1, Heading 2, Subheading, Normal text, Quote) · font · size − / + · Bold, Italic, Underline, Strikethrough (toggle state shown) · text color · highlight · bulleted, numbered, checklist · align · line spacing · decrease / increase indent · page break · link · image · table · formula (∑, KaTeX) · comment · clear formatting.

As built in step 4.4:
- **Laptop toolbar** (the white card above the page): undo, redo · **style menu** ("Normal text ▾", each style in its own look, with its shortcut) · **font menu** (System, Rounded, Serif, Monospace, each in its font; Rounded is Apple's rounded font, System on Windows) · **− size +** (type a size; Normal is 15) · B I U S · **text colour** (A over a coloured line) and **highlight** (a marker): small grids of `DocColors` (made for the white page) with Default / None · bulleted, numbered, checklist · **align left, centre, right, justify** (four buttons, always visible; Lea) · **line spacing menu** (Single, 1.15, 1.5, Double) · indent − + · page break · clear formatting. Active tools are tinted; the row wraps on a narrow window. Link, image, table, ∑ and comment come with 4.5.
- **Shortcuts** (as in Google Docs; in the "?" sheet under "In a document"): ⌘B / ⌘I / ⌘U, ⌘⇧X strikethrough, ⌘⌥0–3 Normal / Heading 1 / Heading 2 / Subheading, ⌘⇧7 / 8 / 9 numbered / bulleted / checklist, ⌘⇧L / E / R / J align, ⌘] / ⌘[ (and Tab / ⇧Tab) indent, ⌘⇧. / ⌘⇧, size, ⌘↵ page break, ⌘\ clear formatting, ⌘⇧V paste without formatting.
- **Typing shortcuts** (after test C, both devices, also in the "?" sheet): at the start of a line, "- " (or "* ", "+ ") a bulleted list, "1. " a numbered list (any number starts there), "[] " / "[x] " a checklist, "# " / "## " / "### " Heading 1 / Heading 2 / Subheading, "> " a quote; `$…$` an inline formula (not money: "$5 and $10" stays text), `$$…$$` a display formula when alone on a line (inline in a sentence); `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `~~strikethrough~~`, `==highlight==` (after a space or at the start of the line, so 2\*3\*4 stays text; no `_x_`, for x_1). **Backspace right after** gives back what was typed; ⌘Z undoes the shortcut too.
- **iPhone:** the keyboard toolbar scrolls sideways: **Aa** · B I U S · colours · lists · indent − + · undo · redo, with **Done** at the right. **Aa** and the colours open their panels in place of the keyboard (the selection stays): Aa has the styles in their own look, then **align** (left, centre, right, justify, labelled, right under the styles), font, size − / +, line spacing, indent, Page break, Clear formatting and **Paste without formatting**; the colour panel has text colour and highlight swatches. The keyboard button brings the keyboard back.
- **Step 4.5 toolbar (Lea):** the laptop toolbar is **one row** of the most-used tools (undo, redo · style · font · size · B I U · text colour · highlight · bulleted, numbered · link · image · table) with **⌄** at the right end. ⌄ opens a **second row** with the rest (align left / centre / right / justify · line spacing · indent − + · page break · clear formatting · formula · comment · strikethrough · checklist); open or closed is remembered on each device. When the window is narrow, tools that don't fit the first row move into the second, in order. On the iPhone (after test B, like the laptop) the keyboard toolbar is **one row that never scrolls**: Aa · B I U · colours · lists · link · Table (in a table) · then strikethrough, checklist, indent − +, undo, redo, as many as fit the width; the rest go to a **second row above**, opened with the **arrow** before Done (remembered). The second row always ends with what goes into the document: **Photo · Take photo · Table · Formula · Comment** (Lea, after test C: no "+" panel, so the arrow shows everything at once). What to do with the link, comment, photo or maths at the cursor comes on a row of its own above the tools.
- **Simplicity pass (before 4.6, Lea; replaces the grouping above, every tool kept):**
  - **Names:** every tool has a name, from one list (`src/editor/toolNames.ts`) used by tooltips, iPhone captions, menus and VoiceOver. Laptop: a small dark tooltip under a tool after a moment's hover, with its shortcut ("Bold ⌘B"). iPhone: a long press on a toolbar button shows its name over it; the second row shows each name under its icon. The bars inside the document (table, photo, selected text) name their buttons the same way (hover on the laptop, long press on the iPhone).
  - **Laptop toolbar:** undo, redo, then five groups with thin lines between them: **Text** (style ▾, font ▾, − size +) · **Format** (B I U, text colour, highlight) · **Paragraph** (bulleted, numbered, **Align ▾** showing the current alignment, **Spacing 1.5 ▾**) · **Insert ▾** (Link ⌘K, Image, Table, Formula, Comment ⌘⌥M, Page break ⌘↵) · **View ▾** ("Pages · 80 %": Layout Pages / Pageless, Zoom Fit to window, 50–200 %). Behind ⌄, what is rarely used: strikethrough, **Clear formatting** and **Indent − +** in words, checklist; on a narrow window what doesn't fit the first row comes first.
  - **iPhone keyboard toolbar:** first row Aa · B I U · colours │ lists │ link, with a thin line between groups; the second row (the arrow) in titled groups **Format · Paragraph · Insert · Edit**, each tool named under its icon (Strike; Bullets, Numbers, Checklist, Indent −, Indent +; Link, Photo, Camera, Table, Formula, Comment; Undo, Redo).
  - **Menus have headings:** the document's … menu on the laptop (**Review**: Comments, Version history · **Page**: Page setup… · **Document**: Rename…, Move to…, …, Delete last; the iPhone's sheet has the same order without headings), a table's right-click menu (**Rows / Columns / Table**), a photo's (**Layout / Align / Size**), Align, Insert, View, Text style, Font, Line spacing.
  - **Plain words:** "Less indent / More indent"; the table bar's "More ▾"; the formula editor's **In the text / On its own line** and "Formula" with a how-to line; "Fit to window"; "Space between pages".
- **Colour picker (step 4.5, as Word's)** for text colour, highlight and table cells: **Automatic** (text) or **None**, the 10 base colours (grey, red, orange, yellow, green, teal, blue, indigo, purple, pink) with 5 shades below each (lighter 80 / 60 / 40 %, darker 25 / 50 %), **Recent colours** (the last 10 picked, shared by all three and synced across devices), and **More colours…**: a saturation / brightness square, a hue strip, the new colour beside the current one, a hex field on the laptop (on the iPhone, **Type code** opens a prompt with letters and numbers; the # is optional), OK / Cancel. Laptop: a popover under the button. iPhone: the colour panel (Text colour / Highlight tabs) and the table panel.
- **Links (step 4.5):** laptop: the Link button or ⌘K opens a small dialog (the text when nothing is selected, the address; "uned.es" becomes https://uned.es, an email a mailto: link; unsafe addresses are refused); with the cursor on a link a bubble shows the address with **Open** (new tab) · **Edit** · **Remove**. iPhone: the Link button asks for the address; on a link, **Open · Edit · Remove** come first in the keyboard toolbar.
- **Tables (step 4.5; after test A, editing happens on the table itself, Lea):**
  - **Inserting:** laptop: the Table button opens a 10 × 10 grid (point to choose, "3 × 4" under it) with **Rows × Columns** fields below it for bigger tables; iPhone: **Table** in the toolbar's second row, rows and columns with − / + or **tap the number to type it** (number pad). Up to **50 × 20**; a larger number is lowered, with a note.
  - **The floating table bar**, on both devices: while the cursor is in a table, a small bar sits just above it (below it at the very top of the page): **Rows ▾ · Columns ▾ · Colour · Header · ⋯ · Delete**. Header and Delete act at once. Laptop: Rows, Columns and ⋯ open small menus there, Colour the colour picker. iPhone: Rows, Columns and ⋯ open action sheets; Colour and Table size… open the table panel. On a phone the Colour button shows only its swatch.
  - **Laptop right-click in a cell:** the whole menu at the pointer: insert row above / below, column left / right · delete row / column / table · Header row · Cell colour… · Merge cells · Split cell (greyed out when they can't apply) · Table size…. Inside selected cells the selection stays, so Delete row deletes all of them.
  - **Laptop hover handles:** a grip on each row's left edge and each column's top edge selects it; a **+** (a 24 px target) appears at a row or column boundary when the pointer comes near it and stays there until the pointer nears another boundary, so a hand that drifts on its way still finds it; a click inserts a row or column there. The handles stay while the pointer is over the table or the margin around it, and go 300 ms after it leaves. Columns can be dragged wider.
  - **Table size…:** rows × columns typed in (laptop fields; iPhone − / + or typed); rows are added or removed at the bottom, columns at the right; removing rows or columns with text asks first.
  - **Room to type:** a table is always followed by a line to type in (added when missing, also at the end of the document); ↓ / → or a click below the table go there. Before a table at the very top: Enter at the start of its first cell, or ↑ / ← (the gap cursor), makes a line above.
  - **iPhone keyboard toolbar:** in a table, the Table button still opens the table panel (rows, columns, Header row, Cell colour, Table size…, merge / split when they apply, Delete table).
  - A cell's colour pasted from Word or Google Docs is kept.
- **iPhone inserting** (after test C, no "+" panel): Photo · Take photo · Table · Formula · Comment are buttons at the end of the keyboard toolbar's second row; Link is a main tool. Table opens **New table** (rows and columns, then Insert) in place of the keyboard.
- **Photos (step 4.5):**
  - **Adding:** laptop: the Image button (several at once), **dropping image files on the page** (they go between paragraphs, never into the middle of a line), or **pasting a screenshot**; iPhone: **+** → Photo (several) or Take photo. Each photo is a JPEG (longest side 2048 px) kept with the document; on the iPhone it shows at once and uploads when there is a connection.
  - **Size:** a photo is as wide as itself, at most the page; drag a **corner handle** of a selected photo (a mouse on the laptop, a finger on the iPhone: bigger handles there); on the iPhone a selected photo also shows **Small · Medium · Large · Full width** in the keyboard toolbar. The width is a share of the page, so it looks the same on both devices.
  - **Layout (after test B, as in Pages / Word):** **In line with text** (inside a line, moving with the text), **Wrap left** / **Wrap right** (the photo on that side, the paragraphs after it flowing around it), **Break text** (on lines of its own, aligned **left, centre or right**). A selected photo shows the **photo bar** just above it on both devices: the four layouts in words with a small picture (**In line · Wrap left · Wrap right · Break text**) · **Left · Centre · Right** (Break text) · S M L Full (not on a phone, where the keyboard toolbar has them) · Delete. Where the whole bar doesn't fit (a narrow window, a phone), the layouts and alignments become one **Layout…** button that opens the photo menu (laptop) or the layout sheet (iPhone). Laptop: right-click a photo for the same as a menu. iPhone: **Layout** in the keyboard toolbar opens them as a sheet. Free positioning (behind or in front of text) comes later.
  - **When it can't show:** a grey box says "Loading photo…", "Photo — shows when online" (the iPhone keeps every photo it has shown, so this is only for ones it never had), "Photo not uploaded yet" (another device is still sending it) or "This picture can't be shown" (a pasted picture whose address doesn't answer).
- **Formulas (step 4.5, KaTeX):** **inline** (in the line) or **display** (centred, on a line of its own). Laptop: Insert ▾ → **Formula**, or a click on a formula, opens a popover: **In the text / On its own line**, the formula (typed the LaTeX way, with a how-to line), starters (**Fraction, Power, Root, Sum, Integral**, put in where the cursor is), a live preview, **Remove** (an existing formula) and **Done**. iPhone: **+** → Formula, or tapping a formula, opens a sheet with the same pieces and a keyboard with letters and numbers (no autocorrect); the formula in the text above is the preview. Both: the formula in the text changes as it is typed; an empty one goes away on Done; LaTeX that isn't valid shows in red.
  - **Formula library (step 4.7c):** ready-made formulas, finance first, in three groups (**Time value of money**: present value, future value, annuity, perpetuity, growing perpetuity, effective annual rate · **Valuation**: NPV, IRR · **Cost of capital**: WACC, CAPM) with a search box (Spanish names too: VAN, TIR, valor actual, préstamo…). Laptop: Insert ▾ → **Formula from the library** (a popover under the toolbar). iPhone: the formula sheet's **From the library** (while the formula is still empty). A pick puts in a **formula card**: a quiet box with the formula's name in small capitals, the formula centred, and under it one line in English saying what each letter means ("*PV* present value · *r* rate per period"), each symbol a small formula. Everything stays editable: a click on the formula opens the formula editor (the legend stays), the legend is ordinary text. The card goes after the line (in place of an empty one), inside a callout when the cursor is in one; Enter on an empty last legend line leaves it, Backspace in an empty card removes it; in Pages it moves whole to the next page. The summary's formula cards (8.2) are the same block.
  - **Graphs (step 4.7d):** a graph the student builds, in any document. Laptop: Insert ▾ → **Graph**; iPhone: **Graph** in the second row's Insert group. A new graph is an example (three bars) with its form open at once: the type as four tiles with words (**Bar · Line · Pie · Scatter**), a title, the axis labels (not for pie), and a small table: a label per row (**X** for scatter, which needs numbers there), a column per series (up to 3, each named), **+ Row**, **+ Series**, × to remove one; numbers with a comma or a point. The graph writes decimals with a comma (18,5) when a number was typed with one, or when the document is in Spanish (a lecture's language, else the account's language); otherwise with a point. The graph in the page redraws at every key. Laptop: the form opens right under the graph (a graph low on the screen moves up first; with no room below, it opens beside the page at the window's right edge, so it never covers the graph); iPhone: a sheet, the graph above it. **Done**, **Delete graph**; a click or tap on a graph opens its form again. Notes under the table say what the graph leaves out ("Pie uses the first series", "2 rows skipped: X must be a number"). The drawing (src/lib/charts, a separate module) follows SUMMARY-SPEC "Graphs and visuals": at most 3 series, labels on the lines, solid/dashed/dotted so it prints in black and white, a description for screen readers. In Pages a graph moves whole.
  - **As you type (after test B):** `$…$` becomes a formula as the closing `$` is typed (not money: "$5 and $10" stays text; Undo brings the text back). A word that looks like maths (x^2, x_1, a/b, sqrt(x), Greek names such as alpha or pi) gets a **Make formula** suggestion: a small chip under it on the laptop (click or **Tab**; Escape or typing on dismisses it), first in the keyboard toolbar on the iPhone. Nothing changes unless it's taken. ∑ stays in the toolbar.
- **Studiply's blocks (step 4.6; SUMMARY-SPEC "Callouts", "Color system"):** what the summary (Phase 7) writes into the document, and what the student can add and edit, on both devices.
  - **Callouts:** a box with a 4 px coloured left border, a light fill and 8 px corners, and a small label in capitals, so meaning never depends on colour: **Definition** (lavender), **Example** (blue), **Exam signal** (amber), **Common mistake** (red; the spec's Warning), **From your notes · 4:12** (green), **You were confused here · 7/10 · 18:30** (salmon), **Added context · not from the lecture** (warm grey). Inside: any text, lists, formulas, photos, tables. Enter on an empty last line leaves the box; Backspace in an empty box removes it.
  - **Adding:** laptop: Insert ▾ → Study blocks → **Callout** (the seven, drawn in their colours); the selected lines go into the box, or an empty one goes in at the cursor. iPhone: **Callout** in the keyboard toolbar's second row (Insert), an action sheet.
  - **Changing:** a click or tap on the label opens its menu: **Change to** (the seven), **Level** 1–10 (You were confused here), **Remove callout (keep the text)**.
  - **Mark chips:** "Important 8/10", "Confused 7/10", "Exam 9/10" in the text (Important and Exam amber, Confused salmon, as the spec's colours; the recording screen's own mark buttons still use their earlier colours until Phase 5). Insert ▾ → **Mark** (laptop) or **Mark** in the second row (iPhone), both with a flag icon (✦ stays the assistant's): the kind and the level 1–10; a click or tap on a chip opens the same with **Done** and **Remove**.
  - **Timestamp chips:** "4:12" in the accent colour. Insert ▾ → **Timestamp** (laptop) or **Time** (iPhone) asks for the time; during a recording they come on their own (Phase 5). **Tap one to play the lecture's recording from that moment** in a **mini player** under the document (laptop: under the pages; iPhone: above the keyboard toolbar): −10 s · ▶/❚❚ · +10 s · the time and the length · ✕. It plays piece after piece (the recording is stored in 30-second pieces). A document with no recording says so; deleted audio (its keep-or-delete setting) says so; while the lecture is being recorded it waits until the recording stops; it needs a connection for now.
  - **Page view:** a callout that fits on a page moves to the next page whole (as in the PDF); a longer one splits between lines.
- **Selection bar (after test B):** selected words get a small bar right there: **Comment · B · I · Highlight · Link │ Callout ▸ · Mark ▸ · Time ▸** (the last three after 4.6, each a small picture and a word). Laptop: above the selection, once the mouse is released. iPhone: below the selection, so iOS's own Copy / Paste menu above it stays (adding to that menu would remove Copy and Paste); on a phone's width the last three make a second row. Not for cells, photos or formulas (they have their own bars).
  - **Callout ▸** puts the selected paragraphs in a callout of the kind picked (the same list as Insert ▾ → Callout, opened under the button on the laptop; the action sheet on the iPhone; "Change to" inside a callout); the text stays inside. Not offered in a table (it would take the whole table).
  - **Mark ▸** (the mark form: kind and level) and **Time ▸** (the time asked, "4:12") put their chip **right after the selection**; the selected words stay. One Undo takes the chip away. Insert ▾ → Mark / Timestamp do the same when words are selected.
- **Comments (step 4.5, inside the document):** commented words are tinted (pale amber, not one of the highlights; paler once resolved).
  - **Adding:** select the words, then laptop: **Comment** in the toolbar or **⌘⌥M** (a small card under the words: Cancel · Comment, ⌘↩); iPhone: **Comment** in the keyboard toolbar's second row, or on the selection bar (the app's prompt).
  - **Laptop, a wide window** (room beside the page): a **margin** of cards to the right of the page, each level with its words, following the page as it scrolls; a click on commented words makes its card the active one; a click on a card selects its words. **A narrow window:** a click on commented words opens its card as a popover.
  - **Each card:** the words (in quotes), the comment, the date, and **Edit · Resolve · Delete** (Delete asks first). Resolved ones leave the margin and keep a faint tint; **Reopen** brings them back.
  - **iPhone:** with the cursor in commented words, **Comment** comes first in the keyboard toolbar and opens Edit · Resolve (Reopen) · Delete.
  - **All comments:** the document menu's **Comments** (both devices) lists them, open ones first, then **Resolved**; pressing one selects its words.
- **The page:** Title is a style of its own above Heading 1 (the document's name above the page is separate); Subheading is smaller and grey; a page break is a dashed line marked "Page break" (a new page in PDF and Word, 4.8); line spacing defaults to 1.5; each indent step is 2 em.
- **Page view and zoom (step 4.5b):** Pages / Pageless and the zoom only change how the document is shown (nothing in its text), and each device remembers its own choice; the page itself (paper, margins…) is the document's Page setup.
  - **Pages** (after test D): white pages straight on the app's gradient, each with a soft shadow; the gap between pages shows the gradient (the dark one in dark mode; the pages stay white). No grey panel and no scroll box: on the laptop the pages fill the centre pane and scroll with it (one scrollbar at its right; the tabs row and the toolbar stay at the top; the title scrolls with the pages, above the first one); on the iPhone, Print layout puts the pages on the screen's gradient the same way. The page follows the document's **Page setup** (below), page numbers where it puts them, and **"Page 3 of 12"** at the bottom left while scrolling (both devices; it fades after a moment, so it never hides text). Where pages break: between lines of a paragraph (at least two lines on each page), between table rows, between list items; a heading stays with the line after it; photos and display formulas move whole; a **page break** starts a new page (its dashed line is hidden in Pages). The PDF and Word export (4.8) follow the same rules.
  - **Pageless:** as before (one long page).
  - **Laptop:** Pages by default. The toolbar's **View ▾** ("Pages · 80 %") has **Layout** (Pages / Pageless) and **Zoom** (Fit to window, 50, 75, 90, 100, 125, 150, 200 %). **Fit to window** makes the sheet as wide as the centre pane allows (beside the comments margin when it shows), never above 100 %; zoomed in further the page scrolls sideways. **⌘+ / ⌘−** step the zoom and **⌘0** is 100 % (in place of the browser's zoom while a document is open).
  - **iPhone:** Pageless by default; **Print layout** in the document's **…** menu shows the same pages fitted to the screen's width, on the gradient. **Pinch** to zoom (50–200 %, in Print layout and Pageless; Pageless rewraps the lines), remembered.

## Accessibility basics
Real buttons/inputs with labels, 44 px touch targets, text contrast ≥ 4.5:1, never color alone (marks have labels), Dynamic Type on iOS, keyboard shortcuts on web.

As built in step 2.3:
- **Colors** come only from `src/constants/colors.ts` (a test fails on any other color literal). Every text/background pair is checked for 4.5:1 in both appearances, icons and the Record button for 3:1 (`src/constants/__tests__/contrast.test.ts`).
- **Dark mode** (Settings → Appearance: System / Light / Dark, remembered per device): the same gradient dimmed by about a fifth (`#8F8BC4 → #9F86B0 → #AA7F7F → #BB8D7E → #C3A06F → #BC8740`) with dark text on it (`#14141F`), dark-grey cards `#262533` with light text `#F2F1F6`, rows `#302F3E`, borders `#454457`, search fields a dimmed salmon `#C99A8C` with dark text. The recording screen stays dark in both; the printed PDF stays light; **the document page stays white with dark text in both** (like Google Docs: pasted text keeps colours chosen for white paper), while the app around it and the editor's toolbar follow the appearance (Lea, step 4.2).
- **Icon-only buttons** (`IconButton`) are at least 44×44 pt and always have a label for VoiceOver; inside small chips they keep their size and reach 44 with an invisible touch margin.
- **Larger Text** scales all text; only tab labels (1.3×), the Library chips (1.6×) and the recording timer (1.2×) are capped, where a fixed shape must hold.
- **Keyboard (web):** ⌥N new document (coming with the editor), ⌥⇧N new folder in the open course, ⌥R record lecture (with the current course), "/" search, "?" the shortcuts sheet (also Settings → Appearance), ⌘Z / Ctrl+Z undo the last move, delete or favourite, ⌘X / ⌘C / ⌘V cut, copy, paste the selected item (⌘V also adds a copied image or PDF; with no tile selected or text selected they stay the browser's), ⇧F10 or the Menu key the selected item's menu (or the empty space's), Esc closes. **In a menu:** the first available item has the focus; ↑ / ↓ / Tab move (skipping greyed-out items, wrapping), Home / End, Enter or Space chooses, and the focus goes back where it was when it closes. Option combinations are matched by key position, "/" and "?" by the character typed; nothing fires while typing in a field, with a dialog open, or with ⌘/Ctrl (browser shortcuts stay the browser's).

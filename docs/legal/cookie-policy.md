# Studiply Cookie Policy

**Last updated:** [DATE] · DRAFT, to be reviewed before launch

## Short version

Studiply's web version **does not use cookies for tracking, analytics or advertising**. It stores only a few things in your browser that are needed for the app to work, so there is no cookie banner.

## What we store in your browser

The web version uses your browser's local storage (similar to cookies) for:

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Sign-in session | Keeps you signed in, so you don't have to log in on every page | Until you sign out |
| Layout and view settings (for example sidebar width, list or grid view) | Remembers how you like the app to look | Until you clear your browser data |
| Editor toolbar settings | Remembers your toolbar choices | Until you clear your browser data |

These are "strictly necessary" for a service you asked for. Under EU and Spanish law (LSSI-CE art. 22.2), they don't need consent.

The iPhone app stores similar settings on your device.

## What we do not use

- No analytics tools (such as Google Analytics).
- No advertising or social media cookies or pixels.
- No session recording.
- No fonts or scripts loaded from third-party servers that would see your IP address.

## How to remove it

Signing out clears your session. You can clear the rest at any time in your browser settings (clear site data for Studiply).

## Changes

If we ever add anything that isn't strictly necessary, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first, with "Accept" and "Reject" given equal weight.

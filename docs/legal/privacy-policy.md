## 2. What data we collect

**Account data**
- Your name, email address and password (the password is stored only as a secure hash).
- An optional profile photo.
- If you sign in with Apple: the name and email Apple shares with us (this can be a private relay address).

**Study content you create or add**
- Courses, folders, documents and notes, including earlier versions of a document.
- Lecture recordings (audio), and the transcripts made from them.
- Marks and comments you add during a recording, and their timestamps.
- Photos, slides, PDFs and Word files you upload.
- AI-generated summaries, flashcards, quizzes and review progress.
- Your messages to the in-app AI assistant and its answers.

**Settings and usage**
- Your app preferences (for example summary level, recording language, appearance).
- A record of when you allowed or withdrew AI processing (date and time).
- AI usage counts and costs per day, used to enforce plan limits.

**Purchases**
- If you subscribe, Apple processes the payment. We receive only your subscription status (plan, start date, renewal or expiry), never your card details. [IF USED: we use RevenueCat to check subscription status; it receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt.]

**What we do not collect**
- No advertising IDs, no analytics or tracking tools, no session recording, no location data, and no contacts.
- We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

## 3. Other people in your recordings

Lecture recordings can include the voices of your professor and classmates. You are responsible for having permission to record (see our Terms of Service). We process these recordings only to make your transcript and notes, and we never use them to identify anyone.

## 4. Why we use your data, and the legal basis

| Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR art. 6) |
|---|---|
| Creating your account, storing and syncing your content, providing the app's features | Performance of our contract with you (6.1.b) |
| Sending your audio, transcripts, notes, photos and documents to AI services to transcribe, summarise and answer questions | Your consent (6.1.a), asked before anything is sent; you can withdraw it at any time in Settings → AI & privacy |
| Sending sign-up codes and password-reset emails | Performance of contract (6.1.b) |
| Enforcing usage limits, preventing abuse and keeping the service secure | Our legitimate interest in running a safe, affordable service (6.1.f) |
| Keeping purchase records for tax purposes | Legal obligation (6.1.c) |

We never make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects using automated processing.

Anthropic keeps data only for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring.

We may also disclose data if required by law or a valid order from a court or authority.

## 6. Transfers outside the EU

ElevenLabs, Anthropic and RevenueCat are in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and otherwise on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 7. How long we keep your data

- **Your content:** as long as your account exists, or until you delete it.
- **Recordings:** by default, the audio of a lecture is deleted automatically 3 days after it has been transcribed; the transcript stays. You can choose to delete it at once, keep it until you delete it, or keep it permanently (after a recording, or in Settings → Storage & sync).
- **Trash:** items you delete stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, then are deleted permanently.
- **Deleted account:** all your data and files are deleted from our database and storage straight away. Backups are overwritten within [X] days.
- **AI usage and consent records:** kept while your account exists.
- **Purchase records:** kept as long as tax law requires (in Spain, generally 4–6 years).

## 8. Your rights

You have the right to:

- access your data and get a copy of it;
- correct inaccurate data;
- delete your data ("right to be forgotten");
- restrict or object to processing;
- receive your data in a portable format;
- withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect processing that already happened).

You can do most of this in the app: edit your profile in Settings → Account, export your data and delete your account in Settings → Your data, and withdraw AI consent in Settings → AI & privacy. For anything else, email [PRIVACY EMAIL]. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (www.aepd.es), or the authority in your EU country.

## 9. Age

Studiply is for people aged **16 or older**. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a child under 16 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Each account can only access its own data, enforced in the database. AI service keys are kept on our servers, never in the app. If a data breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell the authority within 72 hours and tell you without undue delay.

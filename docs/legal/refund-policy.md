# Studiply Refund Policy

**Last updated:** [DATE] · DRAFT, to be reviewed before launch

## Purchases through the App Store

All Studiply subscriptions are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment, so **refunds are handled by Apple**, not by us.

To ask for a refund:

1. Go to **reportaproblem.apple.com** and sign in with your Apple ID.
2. Tap **I'd like to → Request a refund**.
3. Choose the reason and select the Studiply purchase.

Apple usually answers within 24–48 hours.

## Your right of withdrawal in the EU

If you live in the EU, you normally have **14 days** after buying digital content or a digital service to withdraw from the purchase without giving a reason. Apple handles withdrawal requests through the same page above.

## Cancelling a subscription

- Subscriptions **renew automatically** until you cancel.
- Cancel at least **24 hours before** the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.
- To cancel: iPhone **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Studiply → Cancel Subscription**.
- After cancelling, you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. You then move to [the free plan / read-only access], and your notes stay in your account.
- Deleting the app or your Studiply account does **not** cancel the subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

## Free trials

[If offered:] If you start a free trial, you will be charged when it ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before. The trial length and price after it are shown before you start.

## Problems with the service

If Studiply did not work as described and Apple cannot help, email [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We will look at every case, and this does not affect your legal rights as a consumer.
